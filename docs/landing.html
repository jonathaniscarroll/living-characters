How it works

1

Twist

Bend aluminium wire into a skeleton — any pose, any attitude.

2

Build

Wrap in foil and tape. Sculpt a clay head. Paint the face.

3

Dress

Hot-glue fabric scraps into a costume. Give your character one carried item.

4

Scan

We 3D-scan your doll. In minutes it becomes a digital double.

5

Meet

Point your phone at the floor. Your character steps into the room in AR.

6

Keep

Take home the doll — and a link. Your character lives on.
